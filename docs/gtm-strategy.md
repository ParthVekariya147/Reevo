## 2. Positioning

### Core Positioning Statement

> **For local business owners** who want more 5-star reviews without asking every customer personally, **Reevo** is an AI review funnel that **writes the review for the customer in one tap** — then routes happy customers to Google while quietly capturing unhappy ones privately. Unlike Birdeye or Podium, Reevo starts free, requires no annual contract, and works for walk-in customers you'll never have a phone number for.

| Audience | Primary Message | Proof Point |

## 3. GTM Motion — Product-Led Growth (PLG)

Reevo's free plan is the primary growth engine. Every business that signs up for free becomes a potential:
- Paid conversion (when they hit the 20-review limit)
- Word-of-mouth referral (they show the QR to other business owners)
- Social proof (every review posted says "via reevo.io" in the funnel URL)

### PLG Flywheel

```
Business owner signs up free (no card)
          │
          ▼
Sets up QR code in 10 minutes
          │
          ▼
Customers scan → AI writes reviews → posted on Google
          │
          ▼
Owner sees results (more stars, higher rating)
          │
          ├──► Upgrades to Starter/Growth (hits 20/mo limit)
          │
          ├──► Tells other business owners ("I use this app for reviews")
          │         └──► New free signups via word of mouth
          │
          └──► QR code visible at business → customers ask about it
                    └──► Other businesses notice → new signups
```

### Free-to-Paid Conversion Triggers

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| User hits 20 reviews/month limit | In-app paywall: "You've used all 20 AI reviews this month. Upgrade to Starter for 200." |
| User tries to create a 2nd QR campaign | Paywall: "Starter includes unlimited dynamic QR codes." |
| User tries to access analytics beyond basic | "Upgrade to see funnel breakdown by step and device analytics." |
| User tries to add a 2nd location | "Growth plan includes up to 5 locations." |

### Activation Metric (North Star)
> **Time to First Review Collected** — the moment a customer completes the funnel for the first time after a business signs up. Target: under 24 hours from signup.

#### Channel 3 — Product-Led Virality (P0, $0 Cost)

Every time a customer scans a Reevo QR code, they visit `reevo.io/r/<token>`. The URL is visible in the browser. The funnel page says "powered by reevo" in the footer. Every business using Reevo is a passive advertisement to every one of their customers.

**Amplification actions:**
- Add a subtle "Get your own Reevo QR" link on the funnel completion screen (after review posted). Customers who just had a great experience AND used a great tool are primed to share.
- Add social sharing on the success screen: "I just left a review for [Business Name]!" — organic social impressions.
- Display the QR code prominently in print-ready formats (table tent, counter card, window sticker templates) — the physical presence of the QR triggers curiosity from neighbouring businesses.

#### Channel 4 — Email Lifecycle Sequences (P0)

The highest-ROI channel for SaaS is the email sequence that runs after signup. Every action (or inaction) by a user should trigger a relevant email.

**Sequence map:**

```
Day 0   → Welcome + "Set up your first QR in 3 steps" (activation)
Day 1   → "Your QR is ready — here's how to display it" (deployment)
Day 3   → No scan yet → "Try it yourself — scan your own QR" (re-engagement)
Day 7   → First scan happened → "🎉 Your first customer used Reevo!" (celebrate)
Day 14  → "You've collected X reviews — here's your results so far" (value realisation)
Day 28  → Approaching 20/mo limit → "You're at 80% of your monthly reviews" (upgrade prompt)
Day 30  → Hit limit → "You've used all 20 reviews. Upgrade for 200." (paywall email)
Day 60  → Still free, low usage → "One tweak that doubled reviews for cafes like yours" (case study)
Day 90  → Long-term free → "You've been with us 3 months — here's what Starter unlocks" (upgrade)
```

**Transactional emails (already built via Resend):** welcome, invoice paid, payment failed, subscription cancelled — ensure these are branded and include value-add copy, not just notifications.

## 6. Sales Motion

### Self-Serve (Free → Paid) — Primary Motion

Reevo is designed to be bought without a salesperson. The free plan allows the business owner to experience the product before paying anything.

**Conversion path:**
```
Homepage → Sign up (no card) → Onboarding (10 min) → First QR live
→ First review collected → Hit plan limit → Upgrade with credit card
```

No sales call needed for Starter or Growth plans. Checkout is self-serve via Stripe.

### Product Qualified Leads (PQLs)

A PQL is a free user who has shown buying intent through product usage. Trigger a sales/success outreach when:

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| Free user hits 18+ reviews in a month | In-app + email: "You're almost at your limit — upgrade before you lose momentum" |
| Free user has been active for 28+ days | Success email: "You've been collecting reviews for a month — here's your ROI breakdown" |
| Free user visits /pricing 3+ times without converting | 1:1 email from founder: "I noticed you've been checking out our plans — any questions?" |
| Free user tries to add a second location | Direct outreach: "Looks like you're expanding — Growth plan includes 5 locations" |
| Agency / consultant signs up (detected via email domain or self-identification) | White-glove onboarding call offer |

### Assisted Sales (Agency & Enterprise) — Secondary Motion

For accounts with 5+ locations or agency intent, offer a 30-minute demo call.

**Sales deck structure (5 slides):**
1. The problem: 70% of your happy customers never write a review
2. Why they don't: blank page, friction, they forget
3. How Reevo solves it: QR → AI writes it → one tap to Google
4. Proof: before/after star rating for a comparable business
5. Pricing: Start free, upgrade when ready

## 7. Pricing Strategy in Market Context

### Anchor Against Competitors

On the pricing page and in ads, always show competitor prices as context:

```
Birdeye:   $299/mo per location (annual contract required)
Podium:    $399/mo per location (annual contract required)
Reevo:     Start free. No contract. No per-location fee.
```

### Freemium as a Moat (Not a Giveaway)

The free plan is not charity — it is distribution. Every free user:
- Creates a "reevo.io/r/xxx" URL that is seen by their customers
- Generates word-of-mouth when they tell other owners what they use
- Has a 30–40% conversion probability to paid within 90 days (industry benchmark for B2SMB PLG)

**Free plan limits are calibrated to create value before hitting friction:**
- 20 reviews/month is enough for a small café to see real results (typically 1–3 reviews/day)
- The limit hits at a moment when the owner is already seeing the product work — highest conversion intent

### Annual Billing Push

- Default the pricing page to "Yearly" toggle (already implemented)
- Show "Save $X/yr" chip on each plan
- Annual billing reduces churn by ~40% (industry average) — prioritise pushing it

## 11. Competitive Moat — How to Stay Ahead

The competitive analysis scored Reevo 8.9/10 today. To maintain that lead as competitors react:

| Moat Layer | Action | Timeline |
|-----------|--------|---------|
| **Distribution** | Free plan network effects; every QR code is an ad | Always-on |
| **Regional coverage** | Expand to RU/CIS and MENA before any competitor notices the gap | Month 6–12 |
| **Integrations** | Zapier → Jobber → ServiceTitan → makes switching costly | Month 3–9 |
| **AI quality** | GPT-4 tone matching, industry-specific templates (restaurant vs salon vs hotel) | Month 3–6 |
| **Hardware** | NFC badge creates physical switching cost; very sticky | Month 12+ |
| **Reviews / social proof** | 50+ G2 reviews = credibility that money cannot buy quickly | Month 1–3 |
| **Agency channel** | Each agency is a lock-in for 10–50 accounts simultaneously | Month 3–9 |
