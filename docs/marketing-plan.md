# Marketing Plan — Reevo

> 12-month playbook covering brand, channels, content, email, paid, and measurement.  
> Cross-referenced with [gtm-strategy.md](./gtm-strategy.md) and [swot.md](./swot.md).  
> Horizon: July 2026 → June 2027.

---

## Executive Summary

Reevo enters market with a single structural advantage: **it is the only review platform that writes the review for the customer.** This marketing plan builds the awareness, activation, and retention systems to turn that advantage into 1,000 paid subscribers by Month 12.

**Marketing North Star:** Weekly Active Businesses (businesses that collected at least one review that week)

**Revenue Target:** $30k MRR by Month 12

**Marketing Spend Target:** ≤30% of revenue at scale; up to $3,000/month in Months 1–3 (investment phase)

**Core Motion:** Organic-first (SEO + communities + PLG virality) → Paid amplification of what works → Agency-led scale

## 1. Brand Strategy

### 1.1 Positioning Statement

**For** local business owners who rely on Google reviews to attract customers, **Reevo** is the only review generation platform that **writes the review for the customer** — so businesses collect 5× more reviews with zero friction. Unlike Birdeye, NiceJob, or Podium which send a request link and hope the customer writes something, Reevo hands the customer a ready-made draft. One tap to post.

### 1.2 Brand Personality

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Tone** | Direct, confident, quietly clever — no hype, no buzzwords |
| **Voice** | Talks like a smart friend who knows the review space, not a corporate vendor |
| **Positioning archetype** | "The Wizard" — makes hard things seem effortless |
| **Visual language** | Clean, modern, professional. Primary: deep navy + electric green. Secondary: off-white, cool grey |
| **What we are** | Fast, honest, for local business owners, AI that works invisibly |
| **What we are not** | Enterprise software, complex dashboards, expensive, tech-bro startup |

### 1.3 Core Message Hierarchy

**Level 1 — Headline (7 words or fewer):**
> "Your customers write better reviews. Automatically."

**Level 2 — Sub-headline (1 sentence):**
> "Reevo uses AI to draft the review for your customer — they just tap to post it on Google."

**Level 3 — Proof points (bullets):**
- No app download. No signup. Works on any phone.
- Unhappy customers captured privately before they post publicly.
- Free plan forever. No credit card needed.
- Used by restaurants, cafes, salons, hotels, and trade businesses.

**Level 4 — Social proof (rotate):**
- "Average business collects 3× more reviews in the first 30 days."
- "The only platform that writes the review text for your customer."
- "14 review platforms supported — including Google, TripAdvisor, Yelp, and Zomato."

| Segment | Their pain | Our message | Proof |
|---------|-----------|------------|-------|
| Restaurant owner | "Customers love us but never write reviews" | "We write the review for them. They just tap post." | "Collect 5× more reviews per month" |

## 2. Target Audience

### Persona 1 — "Marco" the Restaurant Owner

- **Age:** 35–52 | **Business:** 1–2 location restaurant, 5–20 staff
- **Revenue:** $400k–$1.5M/year
- **Google rating:** 4.1–4.6 ⭐ (wants 4.7+)
- **Pain:** Customers compliment food in person but never write Google reviews. Competitors have 300+ reviews; Marco has 47.
- **Motivation:** Google ranking → more foot traffic → higher revenue. Every 0.1★ improvement = ~5–9% revenue lift (Harvard study).
- **Objection:** "I've tried review apps before. Too complicated. Staff won't use it."
- **Decision trigger:** Sees a competitor restaurant jump in Google ranking → immediate urgency
- **Where he is:** Instagram (food photos), Facebook groups, restaurant owner associations, Google Business Profile

### Persona 2 — "Priya" the Salon Owner

- **Age:** 28–45 | **Business:** Single-location salon, 3–8 staff
- **Revenue:** $200k–$600k/year
- **Google rating:** 4.3–4.7 ⭐
- **Pain:** Awkward asking clients for reviews face-to-face. Tried asking verbally — gets 1 review per 20 customers.
- **Motivation:** Reviews drive new bookings. She tracks her Google ranking weekly.
- **Objection:** "Customers won't scan QR codes." / "Is this too techy for my clients?"
- **Decision trigger:** A friend's salon shows a QR code on the counter → asks what it is → tries Reevo free
- **Where she is:** Instagram (beauty content), TikTok, Facebook groups for salon owners, Fresha/Booksy community forums

### Persona 3 — "Dan" the Marketing Agency Owner

- **Age:** 30–48 | **Business:** 10–50 client local business agency
- **Revenue:** $300k–$2M/year
- **Pain:** Clients ask for "more Google reviews" as part of the monthly retainer. Manual review campaigns don't scale. Existing tools (Birdeye at $299/location) are too expensive per client.
- **Motivation:** Package review generation as a value-add service. Reevo's Growth plan ($X/month, 5 locations) lets him serve 5 clients at once profitably.
- **Decision trigger:** Client threatens to leave due to low Google rating → agency needs a solution fast
- **Where he is:** LinkedIn, agency Facebook groups, Slack communities (e.g., DigitalMarketer), MozCon / BrightonSEO

```

      │
      ▼

      ▼

      │

              │
              ▼
      [Day 14] 30-day results email
              │
              ▼
      [Day 28] Upgrade prompt (approaching limit)
              │
      ┌───────┴────────┐
   [Upgraded]      [Still Free]
      │                │
      ▼                ▼
 Customer        Monthly digest
 lifecycle       + case study
 sequence        + upgrade prompt
```

### 6.2 Sequence — Onboarding (Free Signup)

**Email 1 — Welcome (Day 0)**
- Subject: "Welcome to Reevo — here's how to get your first review tonight"
- Content: 3-step setup guide (under 10 min), CTA to create first QR code
- Goal: Activation in first 24 hours

**Email 2 — Setup Check-in (Day 2)**
- Subject: "Your QR code is ready. Here's where to place it."
- Content: QR placement guide (table tent, receipt, door, invoice), CTA to download QR
- Goal: Physical deployment

**Email 3 — First Review Nudge (Day 5)**
- Subject: "Have you collected your first review yet?"
- Subject variant B: "47% of new Reevo users collect a review in 24 hours. Did you?"
- Content: If no review yet → simple step-by-step. If they have → celebrate + "you're on track"
- Goal: First review event

**Email 4 — Results Email (Day 14)**
- Subject: "Here's what Reevo has collected for you so far"
- Content: Personalised (if review data exists: show count + rating summary); CTA to check dashboard
- Goal: Dashboard reactivation

**Email 5 — Social Proof (Day 21)**
- Subject: "How [Restaurant Type] collected 67 reviews in 30 days with Reevo"
- Content: Mini case study, include review count + rating improvement
- Goal: Conversion trigger (urgency via proof)

**Email 6 — Limit Warning (Day 28 or when 80% used)**
- Subject: "You've used 16 of your 20 free reviews this month"
- Content: Show what they'll lose at limit, upgrade CTA with pricing, list of Starter features
- Goal: Upgrade conversion

### 6.3 Sequence — Re-engagement (No Activity in 7 Days)

**Email 1 — "Did something go wrong?" (Day 7)**
- Subject: "Is everything OK with your Reevo setup?"
- Content: Offer free 15-minute setup call, FAQ, simple re-engagement CTA
- Goal: Diagnose barrier; re-activate

**Email 2 — Case Study Push (Day 14)**
- Subject: "This cafe went from 3.9 to 4.5 stars in 60 days"
- Content: Story-driven email, no product pitch in first 3 paragraphs
- Goal: Inspire, re-engage

**Email 3 — Last Chance + Simplification (Day 21)**
- Subject: "One thing before you go"
- Content: "Tell me why Reevo isn't working for you." Direct feedback ask. Link to cancel or link to book setup call.
- Goal: Feedback or reactivation

### 6.4 Sequence — Post-Upgrade (Paid Customer)

**Email 1 — Welcome to Starter/Growth (Immediate)**
- Subject: "You're now on Starter — here's how to make the most of it"
- Content: Unlock announcement, new features tour (unlimited QR, analytics), quick wins
- Goal: Feature adoption

**Email 2 — 30-Day Check-in (Day 30)**
- Subject: "30 days in — here's your review performance"
- Content: Review count since upgrade, rating trend, comparison to free period
- Goal: Retention, loyalty

**Email 3 — Monthly Report (every 1st of month)**
- Subject: "Your Reevo monthly report — [Month]"
- Content: Reviews collected, platform breakdown, rating trend, "insight of the month"
- Goal: Ongoing engagement + reduce churn

| Metric | Target |

| Onboarding CTR | ≥ 20% |
| Free-to-paid email conversion | ≥ 5% of sequences |

### 10.2 Community Posting Rules

- Never promote without first providing value
- Spend 4 replies helping others for every 1 mention of Reevo
- Respond to every comment on every post
- Share data and genuine insights, not product pitches
- Use "I built this for my own business problem" framing when relevant

### 10.3 Product Hunt Launch

**Target:** Top 5 Product of the Day  
**Pre-launch tasks:**
1. Build hunter list: reach out to 50+ PH users who hunt similar tools
2. Notify all email subscribers, beta users, and LinkedIn followers 2 weeks before
3. Prepare launch assets: hero GIF, 3 product screenshots, 300-char tagline, feature bullets
4. Day-of plan: post at 12:01am PST, notify all networks, respond to every comment within 1 hour

**Goal:** 200+ upvotes → Drives 500–2,000 website visitors in 24 hours
