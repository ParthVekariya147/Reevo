## Quick Facts

- **Product name:** Reevo
- **Category:** AI SaaS — Review Generation & Reputation Management
- **Target customers:** Local businesses (restaurants, cafes, salons, hotels, trades)
- **Key differentiator:** Smart QR funnel routes happy customers to public review sites; captures unhappy ones privately before they post publicly
- **AI models used:** OpenAI GPT-3.5 / GPT-4, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude
- **Deployment:** Vercel (Edge + Serverless), Supabase (PostgreSQL + Auth + Storage)
- **Free tier:** Yes — 14-day full trial, no credit card; permanent free plan thereafter
