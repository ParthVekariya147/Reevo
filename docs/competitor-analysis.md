## Where Reevo Wins (Unique Advantages)

### 1. AI-Generated Review Text — No One Else Does This
Every competitor sends a review *request link*. The customer still has to write the review themselves. Reevo generates the full draft in one tap. This removes the #1 abandonment reason — the blank page.

### 2. Smart Threshold Routing — Configurable
Reevo's configurable minimum rating (1–5★) means the business decides what "good enough for Google" means. Competitors with negative filtering mostly just ask "was your experience positive?" with a yes/no — not a rated threshold.

### 3. QR-First, No SMS/Email Required
Competitors send SMS or email to customers from a CRM. Reevo works at the point of service, anonymously, with zero customer data collection. A restaurant can collect reviews from walk-in customers it will never have a phone number for.

### 4. Regional Platform Depth
Reevo is the only platform in this comparison that supports Yandex Maps, 2GIS, Flamp (Russia), Talabat, and Zomato (UAE/AE). For businesses in CIS or MENA markets, Reevo has no direct competitor.

### 5. Permanent Free Plan
No competitor offers a truly permanent free tier. NiceJob, GetMoreReviews, and Trustmary offer trials. Reevo gives 20 reviews/month on the free plan forever — making adoption risk-free.

### 6. No Annual Contract
Birdeye and Podium require annual commitments. Podium has a D- BBB rating from contract complaints. Reevo is month-to-month with a free tier as a fallback — zero lock-in risk.

### 7. Multi-AI Redundancy
Reevo rotates across OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude. No competitor uses multi-provider AI key rotation for resilience. This means Reevo's funnel keeps generating even if one AI provider has an outage.
